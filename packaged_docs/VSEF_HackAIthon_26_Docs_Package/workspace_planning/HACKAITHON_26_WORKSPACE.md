# HackAIthon 2026 MVP Workspace

This repository is the dedicated HackAIthon 2026 MVP workspace for VSEF. Work for the HackAIthon submission should happen here, not in the original VSEF research repository.

## Workspace Role

This workspace is derived from the VSEF research framework and is used to prepare a competition-facing MVP, submission narrative, evidence package, and implementation plan.

The original VSEF research evidence remains offline research evidence. It is not a production-trading claim, not a live deployment claim, and not evidence of guaranteed profitability.

## Stakeholder Separation

Vietcombank is used in this HackAIthon workspace as the product-facing banking and use-case context. It does not imply Vietcombank sponsorship, funding, deployment, approval, endorsement, or partnership.

Viettel Global is separate from the HackAIthon product framing. References to the Risk Management Department - Viettel Global are preserved only as research attribution for the underlying VSEF research work.

Do not merge Vietcombank and Viettel Global into the same stakeholder role.

## Authority Boundary

VSEF remains a research-only and diagnostic-only framework in this workspace.

The HackAIthon MVP must preserve these non-claims:

- no BUY or SELL recommendation
- no investment advice
- no trading authority
- no broker execution
- no live execution
- no profitability guarantee
- no production trading readiness claim

## Evidence Boundary

Research artifacts, validation summaries, claim-boundary files, and generated reports may be used to explain technical feasibility and diagnostic behavior. They must not be presented as investment suitability, live trading performance, or market-beating proof.

QML is excluded from the HackAIthon MVP scope.

## Working Rules

- Keep HackAIthon-facing documents readable for business, economics, and banking reviewers.
- Keep technical repository paths in appendices or package indexes where possible.
- Do not fetch live data, call provider APIs, rerun heavy benchmarks, or rerun model-universe experiments without a written protocol.
- Preserve active evidence under `reports/claims/`, `reports/results/`, and `reports/generated/`.
- Treat this repository as the MVP workspace and the original VSEF research repository as a protected source reference.
